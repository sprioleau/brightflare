Popular questions

Quick answers approved by the center.

6 answers
What are your hours?

Little Lantern is open Monday through Friday from 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Please pick up your child by closing time.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · Hours
What should my child bring?

Bring a labeled change of clothes, a refillable water bottle, and outerwear for the day’s weather. Infants also need labeled bottles and feeding supplies.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · What to bring
When should my child stay home?

Keep your child home for fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or symptoms that prevent comfortable participation. Call the office if you are unsure.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · Health and wellness
Who can pick up my child?

Only adults listed as authorized in the family account may pick up your child. Staff may ask for photo ID.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · Safe arrival and pickup
Does Little Lantern provide lunch?

Little Lantern does not provide lunch. Please pack a labeled lunch and snacks each day, with an ice pack for perishable food.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · Meals and nutrition
How can I schedule a tour?

Family tours are offered Tuesday and Thursday mornings by appointment. Contact the office to confirm a time and classroom availability.

Read handbook section Family Handbook · Tours and enrollment
